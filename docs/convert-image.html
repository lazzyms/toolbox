    How to convert images with Toolbox

    1. Open Toolbox and choose Convert Image Format.
    2. Add the image.
    3. Pick PNG, JPEG, WebP or HEIC as the output.
    4. Toolbox saves the converted copy next to the original.

    Questions

    Can I convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG?

    Yes. Choose JPEG as the output format.

    Which formats are supported?

    PNG, JPEG, WebP and HEIC.

    Is my image uploaded?

    No. Toolbox is a desktop app, so it reads and writes the file on your own computer. It never uploads your files.
