4. Rent and payment

4.1 The Tenant will pay rent of £1,450 per calendar month, in advance, on the first day of each month.

4.2 Payment is made by standing order to the account named in Schedule 2. The Landlord will confirm each payment in writing on request.

4.3 If rent is more than 14 days late, the Landlord may charge interest at 3% above the Bank of England base rate on the overdue amount.

5. Deposit

5.1 The Tenant has paid a deposit of £1,673, held in a government-approved tenancy deposit scheme within 30 days of receipt.

5.2 The deposit will be returned within 10 days of the end of the tenancy, less any agreed deductions for damage beyond fair wear and tear.

6. Use of the property

6.1 The Tenant will use the property as a private home only and will not sublet any part of it without the Landlord's written consent.

6.2 The Tenant will keep the property clean and in good condition and will report any damage or needed repairs promptly.
