# Settings

Press the `Settings` button in the main window. A modal titled `Settings` opens with three sections:

- **Appearance** (`Choose how Toolbox looks on this device.`): Light and Dark theme options, exposed as checkboxes. Dark is the default. Toggle each and verify the window theme changes; the choice is stored in WebView `localStorage` under `toolbox-theme`.
- **App info**: `Toolbox`, the app version (`Version <x.y.z>` from the package version), and the blurb `Private, local-first utilities for everyday PDF and image work.`
- **Support Toolbox**: `Enjoying Toolbox?` with a donation QR image (`Buy Me a Coffee donation QR code`).

The modal also has `Check for updates`. It can download, install, and relaunch a newer release, so never press it during verification. Close the modal with `Close settings` and verify the tool workspace is back.
